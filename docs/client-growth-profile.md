# Manna Bakery Growth Profile

Last updated: 2026-08-03

## Business and audience

Manna Bakery is a handcrafted bakery, cafe, and gathering place at 306 Commerce Street in Old Town Tomball, Texas. Its ideal guests value scratch-made breads, pastries, breakfast and lunch, warm hospitality, and an unhurried place to gather. The brand is faith-rooted, premium, personal, and intentionally distinct from a generic coffee shop.

## Priorities

- Primary offerings: in-store bakery and cafe, online ordering through Toast, catering, private rentals, and gift cards.
- Priority locations: Tomball and nearby communities, including The Woodlands.
- Priority pages: home, menu, catering, rentals, our story, gift cards, and contact.
- Primary conversions: Toast order clicks, catering inquiries, rental inquiries, gift-card clicks, phone calls, direction clicks, and contact submissions.
- Search themes: bakery and cafe in Tomball, Old Town Tomball breakfast and lunch, handcrafted bread and pastries, Tomball catering, and private event space in Tomball.
- Differentiators: scratch-made food, handcrafted bread, Jerusalem-inspired interiors, a calm gathering environment, Christin's founder story, and the Bread from Heaven identity.
