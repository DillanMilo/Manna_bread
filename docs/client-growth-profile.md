# Manna Bakery Growth Profile

Last updated: 2026-08-03

## Business and audience

Manna Bakery is a handcrafted bakery, cafe, and gathering place at 306 Commerce Street in Old Town Tomball, Texas. Its ideal guests value scratch-made breads, pastries, breakfast and lunch, warm hospitality, and an unhurried place to gather. The brand is faith-rooted, premium, personal, and intentionally distinct from a generic coffee shop.

- Priority pages: home, menu, catering, rentals, our story, gift cards, and contact.
